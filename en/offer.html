FAQ

What exactly does the paid audit contain?

The 4 free checks (robots.txt, extractability, structured data, E-E-A-T) in a detailed version with ready-to-copy fixes, plus real citation detection: 15 buyer-intent queries asked to each AI you selected (Perplexity and Gemini included, ChatGPT and Claude optional), a per-engine visibility comparison table, and a prioritized 5-10 action plan.

How is the report delivered?

Once payment is confirmed, the audit pipeline runs automatically. Within 24h you receive an email with the PDF report attached and a link to its private HTML page (tokenized, non-indexable URL).

What if the report doesn't meet my expectations?

7-day money-back guarantee. Email contact@brozapi.com and we'll reimburse you in full, no justification required.

Why several AIs, and how is the price computed?

Your customers don't all use the same AI: ChatGPT largely dominates worldwide usage, ahead of Gemini, Claude and Perplexity. The base audit (€29) covers Perplexity and Gemini; each additional AI (ChatGPT and/or Claude) adds €10 — €49 for the full 4-engine audit. An AI being unavailable during the audit never blocks delivery: the report clearly flags partial results.

Why one-time and not a subscription?

Existing tools (Profound, Otterly, Semrush) are expensive subscriptions aimed at English-speaking enterprises. CiteScan delivers a one-time, actionable audit in your language — no commitment.
